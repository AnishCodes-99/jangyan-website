NATIONAL EMERGENCY DIRECTORY

Citizen Emergency Helplines & Portals

Every citizen must know these toll-free numbers. Verified by Government of India.

1930

Cyber Financial Fraud Helpline

Report online banking scams, UPI fraud, and unauthorized deductions within the Golden Window.

Call 1930
112

All-in-One National Emergency

Unified emergency number across India for immediate Police, Fire, and Ambulance response.

Call 112
108 / 102

Emergency Ambulance Services

Immediate medical evacuation, highway accident relief, and pregnant women emergency transport.

Call 108
1098

Childline India

24x7 emergency toll-free outreach service for children in need of care and immediate protection.

Call 1098
1091

Women in Distress Helpline

Dedicated round-the-clock helpline providing assistance, legal counseling, and police intervention.

Call 1091
14444

Digital Payments & NPCI Helpline

Immediate grievance redressal for UPI, AePS, and digital wallet payment disputes.

Call 14444
